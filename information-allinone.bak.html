ABOUT THIS STATION

Our Weather Stations in Segamat

These weather stations are here to help our community understand what is happening with the weather around us.

☀️ 🔋
01

Three locations in Segamat

The project has weather stations at three locations in the Segamat area. Together, they help us collect local weather information from the places where our community lives and works.

02

Supported by Wellcome Trust

The weather station work is funded by Wellcome Trust, supporting the collection of local weather information for the HEATCare project and the community.

03

Sensors that measure our surroundings

The station uses several sensors to measure weather conditions. The readings are collected automatically so that we can better understand local heat and weather patterns.

04

Powered by solar energy and batteries

The station does not rely on conventional grid electricity. It uses solar power together with batteries, allowing the equipment to operate independently and continue collecting data.

💡

Why does this matter to you? The information from these stations helps us see local conditions more clearly. It can support better awareness of hot weather and help communities make informed decisions about staying safe.

Learn what each weather measurement means and why it matters.

🌡️
WHAT IT MEANS

Temperature

Temperature tells us how warm or cool the air is. Higher temperatures can increase heat stress, especially during outdoor activities.

Why it mattersHigher temperatures can increase heat strain, especially outdoors.
💧
WHAT IT MEANS

Humidity

High humidity can make the body feel hotter because sweat evaporates more slowly.

Why it mattersHigh humidity can make hot weather feel more uncomfortable.
🌧️
WHAT IT MEANS

Rainfall

Rainfall shows how much rain has been recorded at the weather station.

Why it mattersRainfall helps us understand recent local weather conditions.
🌬️
WHAT IT MEANS

Wind Speed

Moving air can help the body lose heat and may make outdoor conditions feel more comfortable.

Why it mattersWind affects how warm or comfortable outdoor conditions feel.
